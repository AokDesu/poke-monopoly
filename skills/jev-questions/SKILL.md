# Jev questions

Source: spec §5.4 (`docs/SPEC-v1.0-PokeMonopoly.md`). Decision points and their question types are in §5.3; the division of labour between engine and Jev is in §5.2.

- **Role-scoped:** send only what that seat can really see.
  - Public: board, coins, teams, Lap, ability readiness.
  - Own: hand, Balls, peek results, interactions.
  - Never: Event order, opponents' secret items or RNG state. Opponents' Ball counts are not exposed directly in observations but can be inferred from public play (§4.1).
- **Numbers become named buckets**, e.g. `"win_chance": "low"` or `"coins_vs_you": "far ahead"`. Never send numbers for Jev to compare.
  - The state builder is `ai/state/buildJevState` (exported from `ai/state/index.ts`); its input is only the engine's `Observation` from `observe()`.
  - Numeric derivations live in `engine/observation-buckets.ts`, with thresholds in its single `STATE_BUCKET_CONFIG` object. These are presentation thresholds, not gameplay rules.
  - `coins_vs_you`: opponent minus own coins is **far ahead** at +10 or more, **ahead** from +1 to +9, **level** at 0, **behind** from −1 to −9, and **far behind** at −10 or less.
  - `squares_to_start`: **near** at 0–6 squares, **middle** at 7–12, **far** at 13 or more. It is the forward distance on the observed board to the next pass of Start, so a player standing on Start is the full board length away (**far**).
  - `team_count`, `item_count` and own Ball counts (`poke`, `great`, `ultra`): **none** at 0, **one** at 1, **few** at 2–3, **many** at 4 or more.
  - `coins_rank` is an ordinal label such as **2nd of 4**; tied cash totals share the same rank, and badges do not contribute. `lap` is the current Lap label (credited crossings + 1), capped at the target, such as **2 of 3**.
  - Raw numbers are allowed only at explicit seat/board identifier paths, and numeric-looking strings only at explicit opaque engine-ID paths (actor, class, Pokémon, species, item and event IDs). The builder runs `assertNoComparableNumbers` and the JSON characters ÷ 4 size guard before returning; rank and Lap labels are descriptive strings, not numeric values.
- **Chat (`ai/chat.ts`, spec §4.8, §5.3):** `recent_chat` holds at most the last 3 messages addressed to this seat, read only from its own observation (`own.chats`), as `{ from, said }`: the speaker's name and quoted canned English. It is omitted when there is none, so a game without chat builds the same request as before. Topic buttons map to intents in code (`truce`, `trade`, `point_leader`, `use_item`, `hold_item`) with no Jev intent call, and the quote names an item by its content ID, so the state is the same whether or not the AI holds it. A chat between the human and another AI never appears. The §5.3 chat effect lasts for the AI's next own turn only and shifts target and item weights by one bounded bucket (`CHAT_TUNING.bucketStep`, policy tuning, not a game number): `truce` −1 for targeting the speaker, `point_leader` +1 for the named seat, `use_item X` +1 and `hold_item X` −1 for using X, `trade` none (it feeds deal willingness, S10). The shift applies in the fallback policy and the shortlist ranking, and Strategy states carry it as `chat_hint` sentences (omitted when empty).
- **Question builders** live in `ai/questions/` (exported from `ai/questions/index.ts`): `buildStrategyQuestion` and `buildEncounterQuestion` return `skip` (no enabled action), `autoApply` (exactly one, spec §3.1) or a question with `options` mapping each criteria label to its engine action ID (`actionIdFor`). The state is `buildJevState` plus `you.personality`, checked by the same guards.
  - Strategy labels are `a1`, `a2`, … in the engine's order, plus `skip`. The shortlist holds at most 12 actions with `skip`; an action kind is the engine `kind`, with item uses split by item (`use_item:repel`). The personality drops actions whose weighted traits are negative, but every kind keeps its best-ranked action (liked first, then the richer target).
  - **Temporary S06 capability exception (#55):** `buildStrategyQuestion` excludes `declare_pvp`, exempting it from the keep-every-legal-kind rule above, and the fallback policy ranks it below `skip`, so AI seats do not declare PvP until Stream B. The exception, its scope and removal condition are owned by spec §5.3.
  - Encounter labels are `poke_ball`, `great_ball`, `ultra_ball` and `release`. Catch chance comes from the engine's `successFaces` (`oddsForAllBalls`): **very low** at 0–1 of 6 faces, **low** at 2, **even** at 3, **high** at 4, **very high** at 5–6. The Balls of that type held now, counting the one thrown, are **none**, **one**, **a few** (2–3) or **many** (4 or more).
  - Sell Phase: `buildSellQuestions` asks one Noul per offered Pokémon, named `sell_team_<i>` after its position in `you.team`, with its buckets in `sell_phase.offers` under the same name. Its `SellFacts` are engine numbers per Pokémon: sale price now (tired adjustment, no class bonus), class bonus when sold alone and when every offered Pokémon is sold, and keep value (the drop in the team's highest battle-ready current-form Battle stat when that Pokémon is removed, spec §5.4). `keep_value` is always included and is **low** at 0 lost Battle points, **medium** at 1–2 and **high** at 3 or more; it does not forecast Tired recovery, evolution or future coins. Sale value now stays separate: **low** at 0–5 coins, **medium** at 6–12 and **high** at 13 or more; `class_bonus` is **yes**, **no** or "only if you do not also sell a Pokémon that takes it instead". `laps_remaining` uses the count words, and `last_sell_phase` is true once the Final Table Round is announced. Rescue Starters are never offered. Up to 12 questions share a request; more split into requests of 12 within the requests left this turn (3 per AI turn), and the rest are returned as `fallbackIds`. `sellAnswers` maps a request's answers to instance IDs, and `decideSales` sells each Noul at or above the personality's `sellThreshold`; it never reads a confidence.
  - Criteria name the effect (item effect text and names come from `content/`) and every unknown, such as the target's roll. Price Shield is not mentioned while the engine defers it (spec §4.6). Snapshots in `ai/questions/__snapshots__/` record the exact text, so wording changes show up in review.
- **Deal response (`ai/questions/deal.ts`, spec §5.3, #164 D3):** `buildDealQuestion(observation, enabled, facts, personality)` returns `skip`, `autoApply` or a question named `deal`, a Choice with options `accept`, `reject` and, when offered, `counter`, each mapped to the engine action ID. The state is `buildJevState` plus `you.personality` and a `deal` block: `from_id`, `you_give` and `you_receive` in words ("coins", "a Pokémon: Eevee (Tired)", "an item: Repel (effect)"), `value_you_give` and `value_you_receive` (low, medium, high), `concession` (none, one, few, many), `budget_left` (same count words, omitted when the content sets no budget) and `proposer_trust` (`low`, `neutral`, `high`). All buckets come from the engine's `dealFacts` (`facts.deal`); `ai/` computes none and sends no coin amount. Facts come from the seat's own observation (`pendingResponse.facts`) first and the request's `facts.deal` only when the observation carries none. The decision is logged as `deal_response` on every path; a refusal by code over budget logs the reason `over_budget` (log only, the wire still says `auto`). The `deal_accept` / `deal_reject` line is a seeded pick by the proposer's trust bucket (fallback `neutral` while only that bucket is authored), not asked of Jev.
  - **Over budget Jev is not asked.** No listed `accept` (over budget, quota spent, terms illegal) means `autoApply` of the refusal, even when a cheaper counter is listed. Never add a Jev call for it.
  - **`counter` is code's amount.** `counterAction` picks the nearest engine-listed counter in the AI's favour (higher coins when the AI receives them, lower when it pays) and offers none when there is none. Jev's answer is the label only; its criteria say "one small step", never a number.
  - **Wording rule:** criteria say what each answer does and that nothing commits until the human confirms. `recent_chat` is quoted as what they said and never changes the terms (the §4.8 hard line).
  - **Fallback:** `FallbackPolicy` scores the three answers from the observation's own buckets (`own.pendingResponse.facts`) with the personality weights and the trust bucket (tuning in `FALLBACK_TUNING`, `deal*`); it scores everything but `reject` below it when no `accept` is listed.
  - **Eval:** `eval/jev/deal-scenarios.ts` (5 table states per personality as synthetic placeholders until recorded; over-budget states replay with no request). Add a state by following "Adding a scenario" in `eval/jev/README.md`.
- **Keep value types apart:** cash, expected sale value, benefit of keeping the team, risk and promises are separate buckets. They are never merged into one coin figure, and badges are never counted as coins.
- **Wording:** questions and criteria are written **in English**, literally, with boundary cases spelled out in the criteria.
- **One question = one decision.** Multi-item work is split into one question per item and combined in code.
- **Size:** state ≤ ~4k tokens per request.
  - At most the last 3 relevant chat messages, as quoted data with the speaker's name.
  - Memory is included as event evidence, e.g. "broke no_attack on turn 5".
- **Speech brief:** Haiku is sent only public facts, the speaker's own setup (personality text, class hint and difficulty), the world card and the topic. It never gets the speaker's hand, Balls, peek results, another seat's private chat, a seed or the deck order, and never a number it could state or compare.
